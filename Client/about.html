OUR HISTORY

Building a Legacy of Excellence

Founded with a passion for transformative education, QeenEsther Comprehensive School has grown from a humble beginning into a premier institution dedicated to holistic student development.

Over the years, we have continuously adapted our curriculum and expanded our facilities to meet the evolving needs of modern education. Our commitment remains rooted in fostering a safe, inclusive, and challenging academic environment.

Today, our alumni go on to make meaningful contributions in various professional fields, carrying the core values and character they built during their time with us.
